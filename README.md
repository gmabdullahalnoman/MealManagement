# Meal Management

A production-grade Meal Management System for small and medium-sized meal groups.

The project is being developed following industry-standard Software Engineering practices, including SDLC, STLC, Git workflow, documentation, testing, and production-ready architecture.

---

## Project Status

**Current Version:** v0.0.1

**Status:** Foundation

## Goals

- Accurate meal accounting
- Member & role management
- Monthly meal closing
- Market & asset management
- Carry-forward balance
- Reports & analytics
