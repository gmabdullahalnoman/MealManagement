## Core Monthly Values

### For a selected month:
```
Total Meals
Total Asset
Total Bazar
Meal Rate
Member-wise Meal Cost
Member-wise Balance 
```
### Member Asset
   > Member Asset= Total Deposit Amount per Applicable Member
### Total Asset
   >Total Asset = Sum of applicable Member Financial Transactions
### Total Bazar
   >Total Bazar = Sum of Bazar Records 
### Meal Rate
   > Meal Rate = Total Bazar / Total Meals
### Member Meal Cost
   > Member Meal Cost = Member Total Meals × Meal Rate
### Member Balance
   > Member Balance = Member Asset - Member Meal Cost
### Monthly Closing
```
   Open Month
   ↓
   Enter / Update Records
   ↓
   Review Records
   ↓
   Calculate
   ↓
   Verify Results
   ↓
   Close Month
```
***Nothing could be changed after Close a Month, If any adjustment needed will have to be updated in open month.***
