# Glossary

This document defines the official business terms used throughout the Meal Management System.

---

## Asset

The amount of money deposited by a Member to the Meal Group.

---

## Audit Log

A record of who performed an action, when it was performed, and what was changed.

---

## Balance

The financial result of a Member after monthly calculation.

Balance = Asset - Meal Cost

---

## Bazar

A record of expenses for purchasing food or other meal-related items.

---

## Bazar Entry

A single expense entry created for a Bazar.

---

## Carry Forward

The remaining Balance of a Member that is transferred to the next month.

---

## Group Admin

A Member with administrative permission to manage the Meal Group.

---

## Meal

The number of meals consumed by a Member.

---

## Meal Entry

A record of Meal count for a Member.

---

## Meal Group

A group where Members manage their meals and accounting together.

---

## Meal Rate

Cost per Meal.

Meal Rate = Total Bazar ÷ Total Meal

---

## Member

A registered user of a Meal Group.

---

## Monthly Closing

The process of calculating Meal Rate, Member Balance, and Carry Forward at the end of a month.

---

## Personal Meal Entry

A page where a user enters their own Meal.

---

## Daily Meal Sheet

A page where all Members' Meals for a specific date can be entered or updated together.

---

## Secret Code

A unique code used during Member registration through an Invitation Link.

---

## Viewer

A read-only user who can view information but cannot modify business data.
