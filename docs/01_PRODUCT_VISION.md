# Product Vision

## Project Name

Meal Management

---

## Vision Statement

Build a simple, accurate, and reliable Meal Management System that enables small and medium-sized meal groups to manage their daily meals, market expenses, member balances, and monthly accounting with transparency and minimal manual effort.

---

## Problem Statement

Many meal groups still manage their meal accounting using notebooks or spreadsheets.

These approaches often lead to:

- Incorrect meal calculations
- Manual calculation errors
- Difficulty tracking member balances
- Lack of transparency
- Time-consuming monthly settlement
- No centralized record of meals and expenses

A dedicated system is needed to automate these processes while keeping the workflow simple for everyday users.

---

## Target Users

The primary users are:

- Bachelor messes
- Student dormitories
- Shared apartments
- Small office meal groups
- Medium-sized meal communities

---

## Product Goal

Provide an easy-to-use system that automates:

- Meal management
- Member management
- Market expense tracking
- Asset management
- Monthly meal calculation
- Balance carry-forward
- Reporting

while maintaining accounting accuracy and transparency.

---

## Core Values

- Simplicity
- Accuracy
- Transparency
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Scalability

---

## Long-Term Vision

The initial version will focus on meal accounting.

Future versions may include:

- Mobile application
- Push notifications
- Online payment integration
- QR-based attendance
- Grocery inventory
- Multi-language support
- Dashboard & analytics
- Cloud deployment

---

## Success Criteria

The product will be considered successful when:

- Daily meal entry becomes simple.
- Monthly calculation is fully automated.
- Member balances are always accurate.
- Admin workload is significantly reduced.
- Every member can transparently view meal records and expenses.
- The system is reliable enough for real-world daily use.
